# Scientific validation limitations — 17 November 2026

## Evidence classification

- Software verified: automated integration and regression behavior.
- Browser verified: historical saved-case journeys only; fresh closeout journey incomplete.
- Preserved model evidence: registered checkpoint identities and hashes.
- Synthetic known-truth verified: selected 2D/3D geometry behavior.
- Development evaluated: retained development results only.
- Independent reference evaluated: not established.
- Clinically validated: not established.

## Missing evidence

- Independent exposure-cleared unseen-patient cohort.
- Qualified MLD/RVD or calibrated QCA reference measurements.
- Native-resolution CTA lumen references for 3D geometry.
- Independent severity labels and predefined agreement analysis.
- Complete checkpoint-specific XAI faithfulness and randomization acceptance.
- Prospective workflow, safety, governance and clinical-use validation.

These gaps do not invalidate engineering tests, but they prevent claims of expert-validated lumen accuracy, independent generalization, clinical severity accuracy, diagnostic reliability, or clinical readiness.
